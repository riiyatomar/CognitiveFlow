AI Cognitive Aid for Early Dementia & Memory Disorders
1. Problem Statement

Millions of people with early dementia, Alzheimer’s, or memory loss struggle with daily life:

Forget appointments, medication, faces, or recent conversations.

Caregivers can’t monitor them 24/7.

Existing apps are either too complex or too generic (like reminders).

👉 This results in loss of independence and emotional stress for both patients and families.

2. Our Solution: GenAI “Memory Mirror”

A personal AI that acts as a second memory for people with cognitive decline.

Face Recognition + AI Context:

Patient points phone camera at a person → AI whispers “This is your son, Rohan. Last time you spoke, he mentioned his new job.”

Conversational Memory Aid:

When the patient forgets → they can ask: “What did I eat today?” or “What did the doctor say yesterday?”

AI recalls context from the memory log.

Real-Time Guidance:

If patient wanders outside, AI can guide them home (“Turn left in 200m”).

Caregivers get auto-alerts if patient seems disoriented.

Mood + Voice Tone Detection:

AI detects stress/confusion in the patient’s voice and sends calming responses or alerts caregivers.

3. Use of OpenAI APIs

Whisper: Transcribe patient’s speech for clarity.

GPT-4/5:

Convert face recognition/voice notes → conversational summaries.

Generate context reminders in simple, empathetic language.

DALL·E: Generate memory cards with pictures + captions (“This is your granddaughter Priya. She visited last week.”).

4. Feasibility & Tech Stack

Frontend: Mobile app (React Native).

Backend: Python (FastAPI/Flask).

Database: Secure memory log (encrypted).

Extra APIs: Face recognition (OpenCV + embeddings).

Hackathon Demo Scope:

Upload a few “faces” + memory snippets.

Patient “meets” someone → app identifies face, recalls memory, and speaks reminder.

Simulate a conversation where AI fills memory gaps naturally.

5. Why This Idea is Rare & Judge-Capturing

Not another chatbot / marketplace / food app. It’s a humanity-first innovation.

Tackles a real, painful, global issue that is under-served.

Has the wow demo moment: Judges see AI give back memory to someone who forgot.

Uniqueness: Most hackathon teams will chase productivity/education/food delivery. Nobody will think of dementia aid.

Potential future → medical AI assistant + healthcare tie-ups.
